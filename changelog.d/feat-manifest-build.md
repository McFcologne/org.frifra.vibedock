- **Every firmware build writes a manifest.** manifest.json beside firmware.bin and flash.bin says what the build is (board, chip, display, capabilities, how it was built) and holds the SHA-256 of both images and its own checksum. It is made from the custom_* keys of the environment in platformio.ini by manifest.py, the images of the release and of the Docker image carry it, and manifest.py --to-ini turns one back into an environment.
