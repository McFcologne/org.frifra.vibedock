- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 (V2):** a black-and-white e-paper board with a battery connector (200 × 200, no touch). The server sends the picture in greyscale with raised contrast, dark designs inverted (the preview shows the same); the firmware turns it into black and white and refreshes the panel only when the picture changes (partial refresh, a full one every twelve and after big changes); it measures the battery voltage. Prepared, not yet run on hardware.
