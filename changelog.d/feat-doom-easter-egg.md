- **Can it play Doom?** The device card of the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W with the Pico-LCD-1.3 has a small "can it play doom?" link. Its dialog says where the game data comes from and what is packed, and what to do on the board. On download the server fetches the shareware `DOOM1.WAD` itself (SHA-1 checked), converts it and appends it to the Doom program, so no game data is part of the repository, the image or a release. The Docker image builds the converter (`doom/whd-gen`); without it the dialog says so.
