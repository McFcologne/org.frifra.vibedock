- **Portable theme import/export (toward 0.4):** share compact ZIP/DEFLATE `.vibetheme` packages, optionally including
  a coding-agent guide and format schemas. Administration validates, installs, updates or copies themes without
  restarting; selected designs resolve the latest package on their next image. Built-ins stay protected and themes
  or fixed icons in use cannot be removed. Invalid imports leave the live catalog intact. Installed packages persist
  separately from device/account settings; missing packages retain their saved references with a temporary fallback.
