- **Keys and joystick on boards that have them.** The Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W with the Pico-LCD-1.3 uses its four keys and the joystick like the swipes of the touch boards: A pulls at once, B steps the brightness round, X and Y (or the joystick left and right) switch the colour scheme back and forward, the joystick up and down changes the brightness, pressing it shows version, address and server for a few seconds. Other boards do not carry the code.
