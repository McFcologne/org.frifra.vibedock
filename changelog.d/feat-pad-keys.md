- **Keys and joystick on boards that have them.** The Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W with the Pico-LCD-1.3 uses its four keys and the joystick like the swipes of the touch boards: A pulls at once, B steps the brightness round, X switches the colour scheme forward and Y turns the picture, the joystick left and right switch the scheme back and forward, up and down change the brightness, pressing it shows version, address and server for a few seconds. Other boards do not carry the code.
