- **Displays on their battery pull less often.** A display with battery power saving that runs on its battery now waits at least five minutes between pulls (before: as often as its picture can change, usually every minute). A change of the design reaches it with the next pull, within five minutes.
- **The e-paper display sleeps between pulls on its battery (firmware, experimental).** With battery power saving on, the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 stops its radio and processor until the next pull (light sleep, the picture stays) when the server allows it. It never sleeps on USB or while charging, during the first minute and a half after a start, or while an update or a log is waiting. A pull requested in the administration (and *Restart display*) waits for the next wake, at most five minutes.
