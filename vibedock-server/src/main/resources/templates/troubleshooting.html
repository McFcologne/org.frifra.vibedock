Troubleshooting

The display's log shows what it did since its last starts: Wi-Fi, pairing, server answers and why a connection failed. It holds no keys or passwords. What the lines mean: Troubleshooting a display.

← Devices

Display log over USB

Connect the display by USB and close other programs that use its serial port. Works with every board and every display, paired or not; opening the port does not restart it. The log is kept on the server with the display's other logs.

Reading over USB needs Chrome or Edge on a computer and the administration opened over HTTPS or on localhost (Web Serial).
