Request firmware for a new board

A link to the product page, what the board reports over USB and a photo are enough: the display and the pins are looked up from there. You get a package with a report (request.json) and the photo as a download; nothing is sent from here.

The board

What the board reports over USB

Connect the board with a USB cable and press Read from the board (Chrome or Edge on this computer or over https). The board is only read and restarted, nothing is written. Without that, copy the lines from esptool chip_id and flash_id into the box.

The package is named firmware-request-<id>.zip. The firmware comes back as firmware.bin, flash.bin and manifest.json, its version the one of this server with -dirty.
